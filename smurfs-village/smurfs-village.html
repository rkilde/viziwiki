Overview

A whole village raised one mushroom house at a time.

Smurfs' Village is a free-to-play casual town-building game in which the player rebuilds the Smurf village after Gargamel scatters it, starting from a single mushroom house and one plowed plot of land. Players harvest crops, construct huts, and collect resources, guided by Papa Smurf, while fan-favourite characters like Smurfette and Jokey bring their own perks.

It was originally developed by Beeline Interactive, a subsidiary of Capcom, and released on iOS in November 2010 before expanding to Android in 2011. One of the first titles to pioneer the freemium model on mobile, it briefly overtook Angry Birds as the top-grossing iPhone game and went on to rank number one in dozens of countries. The game is built on a time-based "produce and upgrade" loop that can be skipped with its premium currency, Smurfberries.

The Smurfs themselves are a property created by Belgian cartoonist Peyo in 1958. Today the game is maintained by PopReach, based in Toronto, and has been expanded across far-flung locations — forests, desert islands, mountaintops, and even space — through dozens of content updates.
