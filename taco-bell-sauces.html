Sauce timeline

A condiment chronology

  1. 1962 Origin

    Bell's #1 opens with a single house salsa.

  2. 1980s Packets

    Mild and Hot packets standardize across U.S. stores.

  3. 1991 + Fire

    Fire joins the lineup as the third tier.

  4. 1995 Volcano

    Volcano Sauce debuts on the Volcano Burrito.

  5. 2008 Lava

    Lava Sauce introduced for the Volcano Menu revival.

  6. 2013 Cut

    Volcano & Lava discontinued; Mexican Pizza retains red sauce.

  7. 2015 + Diablo

    Diablo launches with a contained marketing campaign — sells out.

  8. 2023 Revival

    Volcano Menu and Volcano Sauce return for a limited summer run.

Trivia

Things only sauce nerds know

  • ✦There are over 30 distinct sayings printed on packets — Diablo's are the rarest in circulation.
  • ✦Diablo is the only tier that includes aji panca, an aromatic Peruvian chile.
  • ✦All four packet tiers are vegan and gluten-free as packaged.
  • ✦Mexican Pizza's signature 'red sauce' is a unique recipe — it isn't a thicker version of any packet.
  • ✦Quesadilla Sauce was briefly removed in 2020; the relaunch was driven by online petitions.
  • ✦A 2015 Diablo launch event in NYC ran out of bottled hot sauce in under two hours.
