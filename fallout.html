Overview

War. War never changes — but the company that catalogues it does.

Fallout is a post-apocalyptic role-playing series set in a retro-futuristic United States — a timeline where transistor-era optimism and 1950s design never ended, and atomic power went everywhere before the bombs fell on October 23, 2077.

The series began in 1997 as an isometric RPG from Interplay's Black Isle Studios, built around the S.P.E.C.I.A.L. character system. After Fallout 2, the property passed to Bethesda Game Studios, which rebuilt it in first person with Fallout 3 (2008). New Vegas (2010), developed by Obsidian, returned much of the original writing team to the Mojave.

This wiki indexes the canon from the wrist up — every game, faction, vault, location, character, creature and piece of pre-war tech — and renders it the way a Pip-Boy would: green phosphor on a dark screen, organised as a directory you can drill straight into.
