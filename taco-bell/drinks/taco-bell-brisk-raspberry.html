Overview

Background

Brisk Raspberry Iced Tea is a ready-to-drink flavored iced tea produced through the Pepsi Lipton Tea Partnership, a joint venture established in 1991 between PepsiCo and Unilever. The Brisk brand crossed $1 billion in annual revenue by 2012, making it one of PepsiCo’s 22 billion-dollar brands.

The raspberry variant adds natural raspberry flavoring to the standard Brisk black tea base. It is available in retail as cans, bottles, and fountain syrup (3-gallon bag-in-box format distributed through PepsiCo Partners).

Brisk Raspberry Iced Tea appeared on Taco Bell fountains around 2015 as part of the PepsiCo non-carbonated fountain portfolio. It was removed around 2020 during a routine portfolio refresh. The retail product was not affected and remains in active production.

In late 2025, PepsiCo announced plans to eliminate approximately 20% of its U.S. product lineup by early 2026. Brisk Raspberry Iced Tea survived this cut in retail, though its presence at Taco Bell had already ended years earlier.

Flavor profile

Flavor characteristics

Brisk Raspberry Iced Tea combines a standard black tea base with raspberry flavoring. The Brisk brand generally positions itself as a bolder, more heavily sweetened iced tea compared to competitors like Arizona or Gold Peak. The fountain format and the retail format use the same base formulation, though flavor intensity can vary between fountain dispensing systems.

Distribution

Channel availability

Unlike most drinks on this archive, Brisk Raspberry Iced Tea is only partially discontinued. The Taco Bell fountain version was removed around 2020, but the retail product (cans and bottles) remains in active production and is available at grocery stores and other fountain accounts including Panera Bread.

Taco Bell

Relationship with Taco Bell

Brisk Raspberry Iced Tea was a standard PepsiCo fountain product available to any PepsiCo fountain account, not a Taco Bell exclusive. At Taco Bell, it occupied one of the rotating non-carbonated fountain valves alongside other Brisk products (Brisk Lemon, Brisk Mango Fiesta).

The Brisk valve at Taco Bell has cycled through multiple products: Brisk Lemon Iced Tea (1999–2020), Brisk Mango Fiesta (2013–present), Brisk Raspberry (~2015–~2020), and Brisk Dragon Paradise (2022–present). The valve rotations are managed by PepsiCo as part of periodic fountain portfolio refreshes, not by Taco Bell directly.

Legacy

Reception and afterlife

The removal of Brisk Raspberry from Taco Bell did not generate significant consumer backlash. Online discussion of the product’s discontinuation comes primarily from Costco customers who lost it from their food court fountains during a transition from PepsiCo to Coca-Cola products, rather than from Taco Bell customers.

Brisk Raspberry Iced Tea remains available in retail cans and bottles at grocery stores and is still served at some fountain accounts, including Panera Bread locations.
