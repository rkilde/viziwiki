Forever up. paragraphs: - Doodle Jump is a vertical platformer for mobile, released by Croatian-American studio Lima Sky on April 6, 2009. You tilt the phone to steer a creature called the Doodler left and right; he jumps automatically; the platforms keep coming; gravity always wins eventually. - It became one of the App Store's first true cultural artifacts — a game that defined an era of one-handed, sketchbook-aesthetic mobile play. By 2010 it had sold over five million copies; by 2014, fifteen million. The procedurally-generated platforms, the rotating cast of monsters, the spring-shoes and jet-packs and rocket-power-ups — all of it drawn like a kid filling the margins of a school notebook. - This wiki catalogs that notebook. Every platform variant, every monster, every seasonal theme (Easter, Halloween, Soccer, Underwater, Space), every unlockable.
