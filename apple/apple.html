Overview

A company built on the bitten apple, the bezier curve, and the relentless deletion of anything that doesn't ship.

Apple Inc. is an American technology company headquartered in Cupertino, California. It designs and sells consumer electronics, computer software, online services, and — since 2020 — its own silicon. The company is structured around vertical integration across hardware, software, and services, a model Steve Jobs called "the whole widget."

Apple's history splits cleanly into five eras. The founding period (1976–1985) produced the Apple I, II, and the original 1984 Macintosh. The wilderness years (1985–1997) saw three CEOs and a billion-dollar loss. The restoration (1997–2007) brought the iMac, OS X, and the iPod. The iPhone era (2007–2011) reshaped consumer electronics globally. The Cook era (2011–present) made Apple the first company to cross $1T, $2T, and $3T in market capitalisation.
