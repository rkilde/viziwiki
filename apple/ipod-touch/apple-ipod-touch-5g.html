Three years. One budget controversy. visual: timeline events: - month: Sep day: '12' year: '2012' tag: Launch title: iPod touch 5G announced. preview: 4-inch Retina. 5 MP camera. Siri. Six colors. Lightning. Same day as the iPhone 5. body: '

Apple announced the 5th generation iPod touch at its iPhone 5 event on September 12, 2012. The announcement covered every major upgrade: the larger 4-inch 1136×640 Retina display in 16:9 widescreen, the dual-core Apple A5 processor with 512 MB RAM, the 5 MP iSight camera with LED flash and 1080p video, and Siri — all for the first time on an iPod touch.

The color range — Slate, Silver, Pink, Yellow, Blue — was announced at the same event, each with a matching wrist loop. The Lightning connector replaced the 30-pin dock. Release date was set for October 11, 2012.

' - month: Oct day: '11' year: '2012' tag: Hardware title: iPod touch 5G goes on sale. preview: 32 GB and 64 GB, five colors, iOS 6. The loop is included in the box. body:

The 5th generation iPod touch went on sale October 11, 2012, shipping with iOS 6. Launch configurations were 32 GB at $299 and 64 GB at $399, in Slate, Silver, Pink, Yellow, and Blue. The iPod touch Loop — a color-matched wrist strap that attached via a pop-out button on the back — was included.

The 16 GB tier was absent. Apple would not offer a 16 GB 5G until May 2013, and that model would arrive without the rear camera.

- month: Nov year: '2012' tag: Hardware title: PRODUCT(RED) variant released. preview: A sixth color joins the lineup. Red with white front. Proceeds support the Global Fund. body:

Apple released the PRODUCT(RED) iPod touch 5th generation in November 2012, completing the color lineup. Like all non-Slate models, the RED variant has a white front. A portion of proceeds from PRODUCT(RED) purchases go to the Global Fund to fight AIDS, tuberculosis, and malaria.

- month: May day: '30' year: '2013' tag: Hardware title: 16 GB A1509 quietly added — no rear camera. preview: An entry-level 5G with no iSight camera, no loop, Silver only. No press release. body:

Apple silently added a 16 GB iPod touch 5th generation (model A1509) at $229 — the same price point previously occupied by the 4th generation. This model omits the rear iSight camera, the LED flash, and the loop attachment button. It comes only in a Silver finish with a black front and is not sold with a loop.

The addition received no press release. It drew immediate critical attention, with reviewers noting that consumers buying a 5th generation iPod touch at $229 might not realise they were getting a device without the camera that defined the 5G's launch.

- month: Sep day: '10' year: '2013' tag: Software title: Space Gray replaces Slate. preview: The darkest color is renamed alongside the iPhone 5s and 5c launch. body:

Apple renamed the Slate color option to Space Gray on September 10, 2013, alongside the announcement of the iPhone 5s and iPhone 5c. The color itself — a dark anodised aluminum — remained essentially unchanged; the renaming aligned the iPod touch's color nomenclature with the broader Apple product line.

- month: Jun day: '26' year: '2014' tag: Hardware title: A1421 16 GB with camera. A1509 discontinued. Price drops. preview: The camera returns to the entry tier. All six colors. Prices fall across the board. body:

Apple overhauled the 5G lineup on June 26, 2014. The A1509 was discontinued. A proper 16 GB A1421 — with the 5 MP iSight camera, LED flash, loop button, and all six colors — was introduced at $199. The 32 GB dropped from $299 to $249. The 64 GB dropped from $399 to $299.

The move acknowledged, implicitly, that the A1509's camera omission had been a mistake. The 5G lineup was finally complete and consistent across all three capacities.

- month: Jul day: '14' year: '2015' tag: Hardware title: 5th generation discontinued. preview: 6th generation announced. The 5G's three-year run ends. body:

Apple discontinued the 5th generation iPod touch on July 14, 2015 with the announcement of the 6th generation. The 6G kept the same chassis and screen, but moved to an Apple A8 processor — the chip from the iPhone 6 — and upgraded the rear camera to 8 megapixels. The Loop was removed. The color lineup was restructured.

- month: Sep year: '2015' tag: Software title: iOS 9 — partial support. preview: Full iOS 9 except Transit in Maps. The A5 held up well. body: '

iOS 9 shipped in September 2015 and the 5th generation received it in full, with one exception: the Transit feature in Maps — which provided real-time public transport navigation — was not available on the 5G. All other iOS 9 features, including the split-screen and picture-in-picture capabilities (though the 5G''s screen was too small for practical split-screen use), ran correctly.

' - month: Aug day: '25' year: '2016' tag: Legacy title: iOS 9.3.5 — the final update. preview: Critical security patch. The last code Apple ever shipped to this hardware. body:

Apple released iOS 9.3.5 on August 25, 2016 — a security patch for a critical vulnerability in WebKit and the kernel, discovered by the Citizen Lab and used in targeted attacks. It was delivered to the 5th generation iPod touch, the iPhone 4S, and other 32-bit devices that had already been excluded from iOS 10.

This was the last software update Apple delivered to the 5th generation. iOS 10, released three weeks later on September 13, 2016, dropped all 32-bit devices. The 5G's software history ends here.
