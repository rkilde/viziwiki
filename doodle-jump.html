▸ Overview

One button. One direction. Forever up.

Doodle Jump is a vertical platformer for mobile, released by Croatian-American studio Lima Sky on April 6, 2009. You tilt the phone to steer a creature called the Doodler left and right; he jumps automatically; the platforms keep coming; gravity always wins eventually.

It became one of the App Store's first true cultural artifacts — a game that defined an era of one-handed, sketchbook-aesthetic mobile play. By 2010 it had sold over five million copies; by 2014, fifteen million. The procedurally-generated platforms, the rotating cast of monsters, the spring-shoes and jet-packs and rocket-power-ups — all of it drawn like a kid filling the margins of a school notebook.

This wiki catalogues that notebook. Every platform variant, every monster, every seasonal theme (Easter, Halloween, Soccer, Underwater, Space), every unlockable. We're trying to do for Doodle Jump what its players have always done — fill the margins until there's no white space left.
